Driftwell Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Driftwell is an iPhone app made by Conor McKinnis, an individual developer in Johnson City, Tennessee, USA. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Driftwell.

The short version

Driftwell does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your saved mixes, your sleep timer choices and whether you have finished the welcome screens are stored only on your device, using standard iOS storage.
  • This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • All sound is generated on your device. Driftwell does not use your microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment card or Apple Account details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. Apple may share anonymous, aggregated sales reports with developers.

Ratings and email

Driftwell may show Apple's standard rating prompt, which Apple runs. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any list.

Children

Driftwell collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If Driftwell ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
