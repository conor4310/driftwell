Common questions

How do I get Pro on a new phone? Open Driftwell, tap PRO, then Restore Purchases. Or use Settings > Restore Purchases in the app.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Driftwell Pro > Cancel Subscription.

Will sound keep playing when I lock my phone? Yes. Use the sleep timer to stop it with a gentle fade.

Does it need internet? No. Everything works offline.

Refunds: Apple handles them at reportaproblem.apple.com.
